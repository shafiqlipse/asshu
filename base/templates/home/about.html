Our Presidents through the years

Symbols of ethusiam, endurance, courage, leadership and teamwork.

2006
Madam Zizinga Rose

Makerere College School

She served with dedication from 2006 to 2012, laying the foundation for the association’s development and recognition at the national level.

2012
Ms. Kotilda Nakate Kikomeko

Trinity College Nabbingo

Her tenure ran until 2018, during which the association grew in membership and visibility.

2018
Mr. Okima Martin Ogoro

Teso College Aloet

His leadership focused on enhancing professional development and strengthening regional engagement among head teachers.

2022
Brother Mugabo

St. Henry’s College

He served until his retirement in 2023, during which he emphasized institutional sustainability and collaboration with key stakeholders in the education sector.

2023
Mrs. Deborah Basekanakyo Wesonga

Wanyange Girls School

Under her leadership, the association continues to champion the advancement of secondary education in Uganda, while advocating for the professional interests and welfare of head teachers across the country.
